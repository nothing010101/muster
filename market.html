Buying, bidding & offering

This page is a read-only view of the market. Trades happen on-chain through your own wallet: call one of the market contract's functions below from your wallet (for example through the contract's Write tab on BscScan), sending BNB with the call. There is no wallet connection and no checkout on this page.

buyItem(tokenId)

Buy a listed Muster outright. Send exactly the listing price in BNB; the NFT moves to your wallet and the seller is paid after the 1% royalty and 1% fee.

placeBid(tokenId)

Place a bid on a listed Muster. Send the bid amount in BNB with the call; the highest bid is shown on each listing above.

makeOffer(tokenId)

Make an offer on a listed Muster. Send the offered amount in BNB with the call; the number of open offers is shown on each listing above.
