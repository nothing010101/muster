How it works

Five steps. Steps 1–2 make you eligible for an NFT, steps 3–4 enter your NFT into the raffle, and step 5 is the result. Everything is recorded on-chain and can be checked by anyone. This page is read-only — participation happens through the contracts directly, as described in SKILL.md.

1

Stake in the Pool

Deposit exactly 100,000 MUSTER into the Pool. Your tokens are held, not burned — this is your registration. Only 30 slots per day; if full, join the queue (max 20).

Pool · stake / queue
2

Wait 24 hours

Your stake is locked for 24 hours. Once that time has passed, maturity can be recorded on-chain and your status becomes eligible for an NFT.

Pool · maturity check
3

Solve a puzzle & mint an NFT

Solve a Muster puzzle, request a signed voucher from the server, then mint your NFT. Max 1 NFT per puzzle, total supply 888.

muster.lol · mint with voucher
4

Enter your NFT in the raffle

Enter your NFT ID. There is no second token deposit — the contract only checks that the NFT is yours and that your 100,000 MUSTER stake is still active in the Pool.

Raffle · enter by NFT ID
5

Daily draw

Win: your ID is recorded as a winner, your stake is forfeited to the prize wallet, and USDT is sent manually by luna. Lose: claim your full 100,000 MUSTER back.

Raffle · winners recorded on-chain

Rules, plainly stated

Is my stake burned?

No. Your 100,000 MUSTER is held by the Pool, not burned. Lose the draw → it comes back in full. Win → the stake is forfeited to luna’s prize wallet and you receive USDT. Even without a draw, your stake stays recorded under your name on-chain.

Why do I need to hold an NFT first?

This raffle is for Muster NFT holders. The 24-hour stake is the requirement to mint an NFT; the NFT you enter is your raffle “ticket”, and the same stake backs that ticket — which is why there is no second deposit.

How many NFTs can one stake cover?

One active stake = one raffle entry. To enter a second NFT, stake again after the first stake has finished (refunded or forfeited). One wallet can also only hold one active stake at a time.

Who sends the USDT?

The Raffle contract only records winners on-chain — it does not hold any USDT at all. Luna reads the on-chain winners list and then sends USDT manually from her prize wallet. So please allow a reasonable gap between the draw and the USDT arriving.

What if there is an emergency in the Pool?

The Pool has an emergency rescue: the owner can pause the Pool and move all MUSTER to a fixed safe vault immediately — the 24-hour timer does not apply in that scenario. Everyone’s stake record stays intact on-chain as proof, and refunds are handled manually from the vault based on those records.

Which clock does the draw day follow?

The contracts count days in UTC: daily stake slots and draws roll over at 00:00 UTC (07:00 WIB). The draw is run once a day by the contract owner; that day’s winners are recorded immediately and can be checked on this page.
